# How HelloMate works

HelloMate helps you keep up with private Telegram conversations. It can prepare
reply drafts in your voice, but it does not send a message as you.

## What you do

1. Connect HelloMate to your Telegram Business account and choose the contacts
   you want to manage.
2. When a contact writes, open the draft in the Mini App.
3. Read it, change it if needed, then send your own message in Telegram. You
   can also ignore the draft.
4. Turn on style learning for a contact when you want HelloMate to learn how
   you naturally write to that person.

You remain in control. A draft is a suggestion, not a sent message. You choose
what to say and when to say it.

## What HelloMate does

For each contact, HelloMate keeps a separate private record of the conversation.
It can use recent messages, a short summary of older messages, useful past
details, and the contact's preferences to prepare a relevant draft.

When style learning is on, HelloMate learns only from messages that you really
wrote and sent in that exact chat. It never learns style from its own drafts or
AI replies. This avoids a feedback loop where the system starts copying itself
instead of you.

## Different people, different styles

Your writing can be warm and playful with a family member, brief with a
colleague, and formal with a client. HelloMate treats these as different
relationships.

- A new contact starts neutral.
- It does not borrow phrases, tone, or habits from other contacts.
- After enough of your real messages in that chat, it builds a short style note
  for that contact only.
- You can review or clear that note in the contact card.

This separation is intentional. Messages from one relationship must not change
how a draft sounds in another relationship.

## Reply modes

- **Suggest a draft**: HelloMate prepares a reply in the Mini App. Nothing is
  sent to the contact.
- **Do not reply**: HelloMate does not prepare a reply for that contact.

There is no automatic-send mode. Old saved automatic-send settings are safely
treated as “Suggest a draft.”

## Choosing an AI model

The Models page lets you compare the current model with a candidate model.
HelloMate prepares two private answers to the same message, labelled A and B.
The contact never sees either answer.

Choose the answer you prefer. Make at least 5 completed comparisons for each
candidate, or about 10 across easy, sensitive, short, and everyday messages for
a more reliable choice. A completed comparison records your preference only;
it does not change the working model by itself.

## Privacy and control

- Conversation information is kept separately for each contact.
- Style learning uses your real messages, not AI-generated text.
- You can disable style learning, stop drafting, remove a learned style note,
  and remove saved facts or example replies through the admin tools.
